## From a scan to a saved record

The kiosk does not show success when it sees a QR code. It pauses, sends the token to the server, and only then confirms the check-in. That distinction matters when a network fails in the middle of a busy class.

## Sessions, not only days

A member can train more than once in one day. Attendance is therefore connected to an actual training session, while unscheduled exceptions still have a one-per-day guard. It keeps duplicate scans out without hiding a second class.

<PullQuote cite="Implementation note">
  A scan becomes attendance only after the server has accepted it.
</PullQuote>

## What changed next

Rewards are stored as data definitions rather than UI conditions. This makes a new title, weekly goal, or return-after-a-break rule a content change instead of a screen rewrite.
