## A different scale

Under water, distance is hard to judge. The view gets larger while the body gets quieter.

> Thirty metres below, the sound changes before the colour does.

The feature format is deliberately open: wide images can lead, a sentence can take a full section, and a later edit can add a video or map without changing the rest of the journal.
